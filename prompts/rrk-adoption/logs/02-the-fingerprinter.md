## Deviations from the prompt

All four rulings are those of the orchestrator's note for prompt 02 (§0).

1. **`STRUCTURALLY REQUIRED`: the pin has 21 pairs, not 17.** The prompt's F3 says 17 "measured at
   `c21cdfb`". That counted `def inventory_spec` definitions; `inventory_specs(factories)` returns
   one entry per registered class with a spec, and 17 definitions serve 21 classes
   (`DimensionfulQuantity`'s serves five). My count is **21**, and equals `test_inventory_specs`'s
   (`test_seventeen_definitions_serve_twenty_one_classes`); the guard asserts it. There are 15
   distinct digests: `version` = `store_tag`; `M_value` = `Lambda_value`; `phi_value` = `pi_value`;
   `InversePowerPotential` = `ExponentialPotential`; `StarobinskyPotential` = `ReclinerPotential` =
   `ReflectingPotential`. The order is `inventory_specs`' (registry order), not `inventory_classes`'.
2. **`IMPLEMENTATION CHOICE`: the docstring says what is true about imports.** The prompt's "loads
   nothing that `config.datastore` does not already load" is not literally true. The probe below
   shows `import config.fingerprint`, after `import config.datastore`, adds RunRegistryKit's three
   modules and the standard library's `getpass`, `termios` and `tomllib` (four modules), and
   nothing of `ray`, `sqlalchemy` or `datastorekit`. The docstring says that.
3. **`IMPLEMENTATION CHOICE`: the golden store has its own builder**, `_GoldenStoreCase.build_store`
   in the golden module, on `_TempStoreCase`, in the form of `_InventoryStoreCase`
   (`tools/tests/test_inventory_report.py:69-109`) and importing nothing from `tools.tests`. That
   module's `build_store` closes its actors before it returns, cannot be extended without editing an
   existing test, and has no row of four of the five potential classes. The golden store has one row
   of each of the 21 classes, five `ScalarModel` rows (one per potential type, each on its own
   potential row), the first tagged `golden` and the others untagged (two tag sets), and value rows
   on the first `ScalarModel`, the `AdiabaticHistory` and the `BBNData`.
4. **`IMPLEMENTATION CHOICE`: the golden file is written once from `take(primary, None)` as
   `json.dumps(fp, indent=2, sort_keys=True) + "\n"`**, so its `taken` is `null`. The test takes with
   `taken={}`, asserts the golden's `taken` is `None`, its `fingerprint_format` is
   `FINGERPRINT_FORMAT` and its `encoding_format` is DatastoreKit's `ENCODING_FORMAT`, and compares
   with `taken` removed from both.
5. **`IMPLEMENTATION CHOICE`: the command-line check is a test**, `TestCommandLine`, as the note
   rules; it asserts exit 0, the `digest:` and `format:` lines (the command pads its labels, so the
   lines are compared with whitespace squeezed), the last line, and that nothing is written.
6. **`IMPLEMENTATION CHOICE`: `TestCommandLine` asserts that `var/` is unchanged, not that it does
   not exist.** The note says "no `var/` exists at the root". A test that fails the day the user
   makes a first registered run would be a defect in the suite; "the same before and after the
   command" tests what the command may not do. At this commit there is no `var/`.
7. **`IMPLEMENTATION CHOICE`: two small tests beyond the recipe** in the guard
   (`test_the_pin_has_one_pair_per_class_with_a_spec`) and in `TestGolden`
   (`test_the_store_reads_with_no_problem_and_every_class`, `test_two_tag_sets_are_read`), which state
   the 21 pairs, F4's "at least one row of every class" and the two tag sets.
