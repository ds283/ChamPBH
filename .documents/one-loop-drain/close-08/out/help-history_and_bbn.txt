usage: history_and_bbn.py [-h] [--phi-init-Mp PHI_INIT_MP] [--alpha ALPHA]
                          [--drain-law {fixed,one_loop}]
                          [--tuning-tolerance TUNING_TOLERANCE]
                          [--tuning-max-integrations TUNING_MAX_INTEGRATIONS]
                          [--fixed-normalization NORM PHI_REF_MP]
                          [--T-stop-GeV T_STOP_GEV]
                          [--small-network | --no-small-network]
                          [--wall-clock-limit SECS]
                          beta M

Run one scalar history and its BBN through the production code, with no Ray
cluster and no datastore, and print one line per stage.

positional arguments:
  beta                  the coupling's beta
  M                     the potential's M, in units of M_P

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --phi-init-Mp PHI_INIT_MP
                        initial value of the scalar field in units of M_P
                        (default 5, as main.py)
  --alpha ALPHA         under the fixed drain law, the drained fraction alpha
                        of dissipation mode, 0 <= alpha < 1 (default 0:
                        natural mode); under --drain-law one_loop, the law's
                        strength A, finite and > 0, required. Passed to the
                        tuning, or to the integration with --fixed-
                        normalization
  --drain-law {fixed,one_loop}
                        the drain law (default fixed): fixed, alpha_eff =
                        alpha w_gate; one_loop, alpha_eff by each candidate's
                        regime, with --alpha as A (one-loop-drain prompt 01)
  --tuning-tolerance TUNING_TOLERANCE
                        the tuning's tolerance on both residuals,
                        |H_J(T_CMB)/H0 - 1| and |phi_ref/phi_min - 1| (default
                        1e-05, as main.py); no effect with --fixed-
                        normalization
  --tuning-max-integrations TUNING_MAX_INTEGRATIONS
                        the cap on the tuning's integrations, the seed's
                        included (default 30, as main.py); no effect with
                        --fixed-normalization
  --fixed-normalization NORM PHI_REF_MP
                        run one untuned history of the model realized at the
                        normalization NORM (the model's own: ln V0, V0 in
                        units of M_P^4, as a stored history holds it) and
                        phi_ref = PHI_REF_MP M_P, by integrate_history, for
                        diagnostics (default: tune the history, as main.py
                        does)
  --T-stop-GeV T_STOP_GEV
                        with --fixed-normalization only: the stopping
                        temperature in the Jordan frame, in GeV (default:
                        T_CMB). A tuned history ends at T_CMB
  --small-network, --no-small-network
                        run PRyMordial's 12-reaction network (default: True,
                        the small network, as main.py runs; --no-small-network
                        for the full network)
  --wall-clock-limit SECS
                        wall-clock limit on the PRyMordial solve, in seconds
                        (default 600, as main.py; 0 disables it)
